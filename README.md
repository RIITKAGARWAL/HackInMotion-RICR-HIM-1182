## 🎯 Problem Statement

### Real-World Context
Most people do not truly understand where their money goes each month[cite: 1]. They earn, spend, and are frequently surprised by how little remains, without ever grasping why[cite: 1]. Standard bank statements are long, confusing, and unhelpful for behavioral analysis[cite: 1]. While existing budgeting tools exist, they are often too complex, overly generic, or require tedious manual input that users abandon within days[cite: 1].

Real financial health goes beyond basic expense logging—it requires pattern recognition[cite: 1]. Questions like overspending on food delivery, undetected recurring subscription drains, or inadequate saving ratios relative to income remain unanswered for the average person[cite: 1].

### The Core Problem
To build **SpenSight**—a web-based application where users can input or upload financial transactions (e.g., CSV bank statement exports), receive automatic transaction classification and spending pattern analysis, and get a clear picture of their financial health with personalized, actionable insights[cite: 1]. The platform operates as a clear, honest, and intelligent financial advisor in your pocket[cite: 1].
